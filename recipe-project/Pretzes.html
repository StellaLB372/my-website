Ingredients
  • 16oz hard pretzels or mini pretzels
  • 1/2 cup vegetable oil or olive oil (canola oil also works)
  • 1 tsp garlic powder
  • 1 tsp dill weed
  • 1 tsp lemon pepper
  • 2 tsp brown sugar
  1. Oven to 250
  2. get small bowl and mix all the Ingredients together
  3. Put the oil and spice mixture over them
  4. Shake the bag until pretzels are covered in mixture
  5. Bake for 10 minutes and prevent burning
  6. Cool completely on paper towel-lined try before serving or storing
